Cinema Documentaries · Since 2016

The stories cinema deserves

Kolo Kino is an independent documentary studio dedicated to the stories behind cinema. We make long-form films about its directors, its craft, and the moments that shaped the films you grew up with.

Months of research per project, broadcast-grade audio and visuals, and a small team of cinephiles obsessed with telling these stories the way they deserve. Built for the way cinema is best enjoyed: on a big screen, leaning back, with the lights down.

The Team

The people behind every frame

A small, international team of cinephiles obsessed with telling cinema's stories the way they deserve.

Ievgen Khalimovskyi

Founder · Creative Director

Ukraine

Founder of Kolo Kino. Writer, director and editor on every documentary. Everything you love about this channel comes from Ievgen: the storytelling, the craft, the rhythm and the look.

IMDb
About Ievgen

Studied stage direction (left in his final year) and worked as a graphic designer and editor before starting Kolo Kino as a hobby in his Kiev kitchen in 2016. He still works from Ukraine, where he kept the channel running through the war with generators, batteries and laptops bought from channel revenue. The creative engine behind every Kolo Kino documentary: concept, narrative direction, editing and the visual signature that defines the channel.

Alexander Mandrik

Co-founder · Director & Producer

Las Palmas de Gran Canaria, Spain

Co-founder, channel director and producer. Runs operations, sponsor relations and overall strategy, making sure every project ships and reaches the right audience.

IMDb
About Alex

A psychologist by training, Alex spent a decade in international business and industry, first as a branch director in China and later leading technical policy in Europe. He brings that operational background to Kolo Kino, where he handles strategy, sponsor relations and day-to-day operations from Las Palmas. His creativity lives in spreadsheets and negotiations.
