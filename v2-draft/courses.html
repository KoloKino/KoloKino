Frequently asked

What people want to know first.

Do I need experience to enroll in the Soul Touch Method?

No prior certification is required. The program is designed to take you from beginner to practitioner, with anatomy and somatic foundations built into the first weeks.

Is the certification recognized?

It’s a certificate of completion from the Soul Touch Method program. It isn’t a state license — those vary by region. Many graduates use it alongside existing massage credentials, or as foundational training before licensure.

How long will I have access?

Lifetime access to all recorded materials. Live cohort sessions happen once per year.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes — installments are offered at checkout via Circle.so for the flagship program and most paid courses.
