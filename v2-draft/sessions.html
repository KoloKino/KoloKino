The session

Not a massage in the way you’ve been told.

The Soul Touch session is a guided experience of slow, attentive bodywork. The first part is settling — breath, contact, a chance for your nervous system to actually arrive in the room.

What follows is shaped by what your body is asking for that day. Sometimes that’s deep, structural work. Sometimes it’s soft and atmospheric. The art is in reading the difference.

What to expect

A session, end to end.

Before

A short message to understand what you’re bringing in — physical, emotional, what your week has been like. Nothing clinical. Just enough to begin properly.

During

90 to 180 minutes. Quiet room. Oils if you choose. The work moves between structural massage, somatic listening, and stillness. You’re welcome to talk or not.

After

Tea, time to come back into the room. No rush. A short note from me a day later if it’s useful.

Aftercare

Hydration, slow movement, no big plans. The work tends to keep working for 24–48 hours.

How to book a session.

Currently I take all bookings personally, via direct message. It keeps the practice small enough to do well.

02

04

Etiquette

A short note on respect.

This is therapeutic bodywork. Respect, on both sides, is the most important thing in our communication. I may ask for identification before any first session — for your safety and mine. I work with clients of any background, but I’m not the right practitioner for everyone. If we’re not a fit, I’ll tell you, and I’ll point you to someone better suited where I can.
