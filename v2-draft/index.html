Meet Soul

Hands trained by curiosity. A method born from listening.

I’ve spent years studying the body — not as a machine to fix, but as a language to learn. The Soul Touch Method is what came out of that study: a way of working with the body that is precise, attuned, and unafraid of tenderness.

Whether you come here as a client, a student, or someone simply curious — what I offer is the same: a serious craft, taught seriously, in a space that respects what touch really is.
