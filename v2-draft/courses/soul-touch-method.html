Who it’s for

Bodyworkers who want depth, not just technique.

· Massage therapists looking to deepen their practice with somatic and nervous-system work.

· Bodyworkers, dancers, and movement teachers who already work with bodies and want a more attuned approach.

· Beginners who are serious — and willing to do the work to start in the right place.

· Practitioners who suspect their training taught them mechanics but not presence.

Curriculum

Eight weeks. Eight movements.

Each week builds on the last. Lessons are released weekly during the cohort, and remain available forever after.

01

Foundations

Setting up the space. Centering yourself. Reading first contact. The architecture of a session.

02

Anatomy of touch

Fascia, nervous system, breath. What your hands are actually meeting.

03

Listening hands

Palpation as conversation. Reading restriction, tone, and willingness.

04

Pressure & pace

The two variables that change everything. Drills, exercises, partner work.

05

Working with the nervous system

Down-regulation, co-regulation, and the somatic shift you’re actually working toward.

06

Trauma-informed practice

Holding space without becoming a therapist. Knowing what is yours to do, and what isn’t.

07

The full session

Putting it together. Sequencing. Closing. What to do when nothing happens.

08

Practice & certification

Submitted practice sessions, feedback, and certificate.
