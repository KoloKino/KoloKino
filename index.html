Brand Partnerships

Sponsorship that fits the story

We integrate sponsors into the documentary's narrative — not against it. The result: long-form attention from a high-intent audience.

◐

Story-driven integration

Your brand becomes part of the documentary's arc, not a 60-second interruption between scenes. We write, voice and edit each integration as a natural beat in the story.

How it works
We discuss your brand's voice, values and audience. We then craft a 60–120s segment that feels like the narrator quoting a relevant fact — not a commercial. Final cuts are reviewed with you before publication. This approach is why our viewers don't skip: the integration is the show.
◑

Long-form watch time

6.5 million hours watched lifetime. Our average video is 14 minutes — sponsors get viewers who chose to spend real time with the content.

Why this matters
Short-form ads compete with thumb-scrolls. We compete with movies. 52% of our watch time happens on living-room TVs (Smart TVs, Roku, Apple TV, Fire TV) — the same setting people watch Netflix in, with the same attention level. That is rare on YouTube and almost unheard of among independent creators.
◓

Evergreen visibility

Documentaries don't expire. Every Oscar, every new release, every anniversary brings a fresh wave of viewers back to the relevant film — and back to your sponsorship.

See the DiCaprio effect
Our Leonardo DiCaprio documentary (1.9M views) keeps spiking years after release whenever a new DiCaprio film drops or awards season comes around. The chart below tracks views over time — every peak is a free distribution event for the sponsor of that video.
