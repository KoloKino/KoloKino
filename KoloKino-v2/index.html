The stories cinema deserves

Kolo Kino is an independent documentary studio dedicated to the stories behind cinema. We make long-form films about its directors, its craft, and the moments that shaped the films you grew up with. Months of research per project, broadcast-grade audio and visuals, and a small team of cinephiles obsessed with telling these stories the way they deserve. Built for the way cinema is best enjoyed: on a big screen, leaning back, with the lights down.

SheetBrand Partnerships

Sponsorship that fits the story

Story-driven
Evergreen reach

We integrate sponsors into the documentary's narrative, not against it. The result: long-form attention from a high-intent audience.

◐

Story-driven integration

Your brand becomes part of the documentary's arc, not a 60-second interruption between scenes. We write, voice and edit each integration as a natural beat in the story.

How it works
We discuss your brand's voice, values and audience. We then craft a 60 to 120-second segment that feels like the narrator quoting a relevant fact, not a commercial. Final cuts are reviewed with you before publication. This approach is why our viewers don't skip: the integration is the show.
◑

Long-form watch time

6.5 million hours watched lifetime. Our average video is 14 minutes long, so sponsors get viewers who chose to spend real time with the content.

Why this matters
Short-form ads compete with thumb-scrolls. We compete with movies. 52% of our watch time happens on living-room TVs (Smart TVs, Roku, Apple TV, Fire TV), the same setting people watch Netflix in, with the same attention level. That is rare on YouTube and almost unheard of among independent creators.
◓

Evergreen visibility

Documentaries don't expire. Every Oscar, every new release, every anniversary brings a fresh wave of viewers back to the relevant film, and back to your sponsorship.
